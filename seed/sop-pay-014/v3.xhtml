Purpose & scope

This procedure governs a planned switchover of production card authorisation traffic from the primary card processor to the backup processor at Northwind Pay. It applies to every change ticket in the CHG project that moves live payment traffic.

Roles

  • Change implementer: Payments SRE on call
  • Change approver: Head of Payments Operations

Pre-checks

  1. Traffic is below 2,000 transactions per second.
  2. On-call engineer has acknowledged the change window.
  3. Status page draft is prepared.
  4. Backup processor health check is green.

Switchover

  1. Announce the start of the change in #payments-ops.
  2. Set the processor-routing weight to 10% backup and hold for 5 minutes.
  3. Increase the backup weight to 50% and hold for 10 minutes.
  4. Increase the backup weight to 100%.
  5. Confirm the authorisation rate on the backup procesor is at or above 95% for 10 minutes.

Rollback

If the authorisation rate drops below 92% for 5 minutes, revert DNS weight to the primary processor. Complete the rollback within 30 minutes.

Post-implementation review

  1. Attach authorisation-rate graphs covering 30 minutes before and after the switchover to the change ticket.
  2. Record any customer-impacting errors and the incident number, if one was raised.
  3. The change approver confirms closure in the change ticket.
